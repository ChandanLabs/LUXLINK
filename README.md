# Luxlink

**See the page before you trust the link.**

Luxlink is a privacy-first link safety gateway. It scans a public destination, surfaces its redirect chain and risk signals, and gives people a preview before they choose to continue.

## Live deployment

[Open Luxlink on Vercel](https://luxlink-8ft40hf6f-chandanlabs-projects.vercel.app)

> Vercel deployment protection may ask you to sign in before opening the app.

## What it does

- Scans public links and displays page details, redirect hops, tracker counts, and risk signals.
- Creates shareable gateways with optional passwords, custom names, and expiration or one-time opening.
- Keeps the link vault encrypted in the browser, with import and export.
- Offers QR codes, share cards, and privacy-minded click analytics without storing IP addresses.

## Built with

React, TypeScript, TanStack Start, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and PostgreSQL/PGlite.
